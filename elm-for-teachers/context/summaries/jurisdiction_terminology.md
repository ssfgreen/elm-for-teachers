# Jurisdiction Terminology and Statutory Frames (data context)

**Type:** Reference document for agentic systems. Read whenever a task mentions SEN, SEND, ASN, ASL, ALN, inclusion, support plans or statutory duties, or when the jurisdiction is known.
**Version:** 0.1, 16 October 2026. Statutory references should be re-verified against the current GOV.UK, gov.scot and gov.wales texts before each pilot phase.

## Where the line sits between routine adaptive teaching and additional provision

This matters for an agent because tasks on the "routine" side are delegable and tasks on the "additional" side are statutory and human-only.

- **England:** "High quality teaching, differentiated for individual pupils, is the first step in responding to pupils who have or may have SEN" (Code of Practice 6.37). SEN support begins when the pupil needs provision additional to or different from that. An agent may help with the first step; it should not write EHC plan content.
- **Scotland:** Needs-led. The agent should take a description of the barrier, not a category. Many pupils with ASN have no diagnosis and no CSP. GIRFEC's wellbeing indicators (Safe, Healthy, Achieving, Nurtured, Active, Respected, Responsible, Included) are the planning language.
- **Wales:** The ALN Code states that differentiated teaching is a fundamental element of high-quality routine teaching and does not itself constitute additional learning provision. The agent is therefore squarely in the routine space; IDP content is human-only.
- **Northern Ireland:** Not reviewed in depth in this version. Treat as England-like with its own Code; verify before use.

## Phrases to use and avoid

| Use | Avoid | Why |
|---|---|---|
| "pupil with dyslexia", "autistic pupil" (ask the school's preference) | "dyslexic", "ASD child" | Identity-first vs person-first varies by community; autism communities often prefer identity-first |
| "barrier", "support need" | "deficit", "lacks", "can't" | Barrier framing matches the statutory definitions and the EEF guidance |
| "support level", "current mastery" | "ability", "low ability", "bottom set" | Fixed-ability language has negative evidence (see negative_evidence_register.md) |
| "pupil with English as an additional language" | "EAL kids", treating EAL as SEN | EAL is a language status, not a learning difficulty |
| "ASN" in Scotland, "ALN" in Wales | "SEN" everywhere | Teachers notice |

# AI and Philosophy

A lesson about the philosophical questions arising from the development of AI.

Learners will be able to:

- Identify some of the philosophical questions raised by AI.
- Explain the differences and similarities between humans and AI.
- Create a test to determine what is an AI or human response.

This lesson was developed by Ewen Glen.

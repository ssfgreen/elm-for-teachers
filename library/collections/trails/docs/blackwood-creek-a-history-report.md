# Blackwood Creek: A History Report

Blackwood Creek is an educational, browser-based, “choose-your-own-adventure” style activity designed to help students thoughtfully explore the role of artificial intelligence in completing assignments. Through interactive storytelling, players will make critical decisions and experience the potential consequences of using AI in their academic work.

Activity developed by Rayo Verweij and Callum Glasgow.

# Children’s Rights & AI Teaching Pack

Our friends at the Scottish AI Alliance and Children’s Parliament have created a set of lessons aimed at upper Primary learners.

Developed with children and educators across Scotland, the resource pack introduces AI in a way that’s practical, ethical and age-appropriate, with children’s human rights at its core.

Featuring six ready-to-use lesson plans, real-life scenarios, and animated videos created by children, the pack supports teachers to explore AI in fun, thoughtful and meaningful ways.

You can freely access and download the resources directly from their website using the link below.

[https://www.scottishai.com/children-and-ai/teachingpack](https://www.scottishai.com/children-and-ai/teachingpack)

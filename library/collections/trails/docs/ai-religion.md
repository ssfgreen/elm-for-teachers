# AI and Religion

A lesson exploring the consequences of AI for religious beliefs and practices, using GenAI as a learning tool.

In this lesson, learners will:

- Identify some of the impacts AI might have on religious belief and practice

- Explain how the values of Christianity might contribute to society’s use of AI

- Consider the responses of world religions to AI and the relevance of these to their own beliefs and values.

This lesson was developed by Ewen Glen.

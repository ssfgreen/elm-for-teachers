## Slide 15

How does image generation work?

Image generation models are a type of Generative AI used to create pictures and artwork.

These models are trained on millions of images, many of which have labels or captions (e.g., "dog," "sunset"). The labels help the AI learn how to connect words to pictures.

When you give a description (e.g., “a dragon flying over a castle at sunset”), the model uses what it has learned to create a brand-new image that matches your request.

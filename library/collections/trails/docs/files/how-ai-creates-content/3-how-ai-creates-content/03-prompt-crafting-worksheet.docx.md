# 03 Prompt crafting worksheet

From the TRAILS resource [How AI creates content](https://trails.scot/resource/how-ai-creates-content/) (DOCX, in 3.-How-AI-creates-content.zip).

GenAI Prompt Crafting

This worksheet will help you plan a detailed prompt to generate the image you want of your ideal school of the future.

Subject:

What do you want in your image?

Type of image:

Photo, artwork, cartoon, drawing

Setting:

Indoors, outdoors, a real or imaginary location, a historical time period, or in the future

Mood/atmosphere: Fun, spooky, magical, etc.

Time of day: Dawn, midday, night time, etc.

Scene elements:

Objects in the scene or background, like trees, river, table, bookshelves

Framing:

Close-up, wide shot, landscape etc.

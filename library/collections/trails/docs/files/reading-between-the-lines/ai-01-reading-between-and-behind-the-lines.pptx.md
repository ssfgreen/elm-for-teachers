TRAIL.scot

Reading Between and Behind the Lines:

Using AI to understand the hidden messages in photos and pictures

In this lesson, we will use AI as a “thinking partner” to help us understand hidden messages and bias in news images.

Working with AI can help us build stronger thinking skills and look more closely at images in the media.

## Slide 2

What we are learning:

We are learning how to use AI to analyse and evaluate hidden messages in news images.

TRAIL.scot

Learning Intention and Success Criteria

How we know we have learned it:

I can describe what I notice in a news image.

I can use AI to explore points of view.

I can use a bias scale to think about how an image might affect opinions.

I can explain how bias might affect how someone understands an event.

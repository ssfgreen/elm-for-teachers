## Page 38

What is bias?
Bias is a feeling or judgement you make about someone.
Bias is often based on assumptions we make about
people. Assumptions are ideas or thoughts we have that aren't
based on facts.
For example: there are no boys on a school's eco
committee. The teacher in charge assumes that this is because
boys aren't interested in helping the environment.
Discussion: how might this assumption lead to the teacher being
biased against boys?

Types of bias
Some of the things that people can be biased about are:
- Where someone is from
- Their skin colour
- Their accent or what language they speak
- Their religion
- Their gender
- Who they choose to love or marry
- A person's abilities or disabilities
Discussion: have you ever experienced bias because of anything listed
above? Is there anything missing from the list?

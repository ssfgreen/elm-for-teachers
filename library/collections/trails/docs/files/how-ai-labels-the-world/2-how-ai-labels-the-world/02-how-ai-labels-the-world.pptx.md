## Slide 4

What are Classification Systems?

Classification/Classifier Systems are types of AI systems that categorise or identify data based on learned patterns.

They use machine learning models which are trained on large datasets to recognise patterns and make predictions.

AI, is this a cat or a dog?

## Slide 5

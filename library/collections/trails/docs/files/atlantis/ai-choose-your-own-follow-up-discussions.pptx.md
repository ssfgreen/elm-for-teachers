‘Hugging Face’ AI Energy Scores

## Slide 15

Why can’t we use sea water?

The salt and minerals in sea water are corrosive, so it is difficult to work with.

The water also needs to be cold enough to cool the servers!

Swimming pools heated by servers? Really?!

Do undersea data centres exist?

Do AI servers really need that much water?

Back to the questions

Water is also needed for making the semiconductors in AI systems. An average chip factory can use as much water as 33,000 households every day. This water must be ‘ultrapure’, not salty sea water.

There are data centres in Finland and Sweden cooled by sea water.

They also have to watch out for jellyfish getting sucked in with the water! (source)

A report from Google said that 78% of the water used in their data centres worldwide is ‘potable’ (drinking water). (source)

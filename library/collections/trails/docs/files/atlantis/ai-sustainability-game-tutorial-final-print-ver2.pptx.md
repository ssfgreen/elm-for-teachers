# AI Sustainability Game tutorial slides

From the TRAILS resource [Atlantis Island AI Sustainability Game](https://trails.scot/resource/atlantis/) (PPTX).

## Slide 1

How to play

## Slide 2

Atlantis Island, long thought to be lost to the oceans, has actually been safe but hidden all these centuries.

**Notes:** The aim of the board game is for all players to set up and run their own tech business to digitise and share all the knowledge of Atlantis, go from simple websites to social media to AI services and to get to the end of 12 rounds without destroying Atlantis Island or its inhabitants.

## Slide 3

The Atlanteans have decided to come out of hiding and share their knowledge in the hope that it might benefit the planet.

**Notes:** The aim of the board game is for all players to set up and run their own tech business to digitise and share all the knowledge of Atlantis, go from simple websites to social media to AI services and to get to the end of 12 rounds without destroying Atlantis Island or its inhabitants.

## Slide 4

You have been invited to the island to set up a data centre to digitise the libraries, galleries, theatres, news and marketplaces of Atlantis.

**Notes:** Atlantis Island has survived thanks to the islanders’ careful balance and harmony with nature. Try not to use too much water, so there is always enough drinking water. Try not to use too much power, so there is always enough to power the hospital and homes, and keep the sea barriers machinery working otherwise Atlantis would sink. Try to not pollute the air by using too much fossil fuel too, so that the islanders can breathe clean air.

## Slide 5

Work together to upload and share as much data as you can… without damaging the island.

**Notes:** At the start of the game you will choose a Web 1.0 web server. You can choose to focus initially on digitising books, images, videos, news or shop items. You will gather data and earn money as more people access your data and servers. You can then save up for additional servers, including Web 2.0 (which are social media style) servers, and finally Web 3.0 servers that use AI. Getting more Web 1.0 and Web 2.0 servers give you more dice to roll (so you can gather more data).

## Slide 6

Data Centres for web servers and AI services need three main things:

Data: huge amounts of data is needed to train AI systems, but websites need data too.

Power: electricity is needed to run the servers. Data centres need lots of power!

Water: Servers get very hot. Water cooling is more effective than air cooling

There is no winner of the game!

## Slide 10

Setup

Each player gets a board with their data centre, servers and bank.

You will need a pen and some dice.

**Notes:** If you roll 1-5 then fill in1-5 circles in your data repository. You can choose to fill in circles of any data type (books, images, video etc.) although you might want to focus on gaining data in the same row as your Web 1.0 server(s) to be able to expand later.

## Slide 11

**Notes:** If you fill in one of the coin circles, you have earned one coin. Colour in one coin in your bank for every coin you earn in your data centre.

## Slide 17

Playing the game

You start with one ‘data dice’

You can get more ‘data dice’ by buying more servers

**Notes:** Each round you will need to reduce the water level depending on the water requirements of your servers. For example, if you have one Gen1 server, reduce the water level by the two water drops shown on that server. In the first round, the four players will need to reduce the reservoir by 8 water drops. Each round count up how much water all your servers need. Pull the reservoir water level down by that many water drops.

## Slide 18

Gather dataand earn money

Roll your dice and add the data to your ‘Data Centre’

If you roll a 1, 2, 3, 4 or 5, fill in that many data points

**Notes:** Each round, one player should roll two dice to find out how much rain has fallen and filled up the island’s reservoir. Move the water level up by that number. For example, if the dice rolled were a 2 and a 3, move the water level up by 5.

## Slide 19

If you roll a 3, fill in 3 data points

If you colour in coins in your data centre, add that many coins to your bank

Gather data and earn money

**Notes:** Check to see if there is still power and water. If the water level is at zero or there are no free power spots then the islanders die. If there are three pollution clouds filled in, then the islanders die. If a player has purchased the nuclear power plant, they should roll two dice. If two 6s are rolled, then the nuclear power plant has an accident and the islanders die.

**Notes:** Teacher Guide: Introduce another example of using AI for summarisation, this time with a political policy (the Assisted Dying Bill in the UK). Facilitate a discussion on the accuracy and completeness of the AI summaries from both historical and political examples. Within this discussion consider the modern political implications. Could AI replace politicians and human input in policy making? Could AI make laws that support humanity? Suggested AI tools include: ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini, Brisk

## Slide 11

Summarising with AI

AI can be used to edit content in numerous ways.

One way is to summarise content. Let’s start with the Scottish Wars of Independence…

Find a complex historical source and try it yourself.

**Notes:** Teacher Guide: Pose the question: "When we use AI to summarise what should we do next? Should we just accept the final output?". Lead a discussion emphasizing the importance of not blindly accepting AI output and the need for critical evaluation. Instruct students to "Make a three-step plan of what you would do after AI generates a summary for you". Encourage them to consider steps like: Checking for factual accuracy. Comparing with original sources. Identifying potential biases or omissions.

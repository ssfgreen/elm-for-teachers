# Responsible GenAI in Schools cards

We’ve created a set of printable cards which teachers can use with upper primary school children to help them think about the uses of GenAI in schools. The cards are based on the text of our [AI Jungle Book](https://dataschools.education/exploring-the-ai-jungle/) but they are 100% metaphor-free 🙂 for learners who get on better with literal language.

Here’s a [video](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/dog-and-fox_what-do-your-kids-think-genai-cards-for-activity-7378337756155940865-VhP8?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAAAIEDNIBr3kXKABh63AWuGyH_z1SF3tFW4o) about them from our excellent graphic designer Elspeth Maxwell from Dog and Fox Design.

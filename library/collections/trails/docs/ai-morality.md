# AI and Morality

A lesson exploring how AI is used, can be used, and should be used in moral decision making.  
Learners will:  
– Consider if AI can be trusted to make moral decisions.  
– Consider if AI can be used to promote a more just and compassionate society  
– Analyse the benefits and drawbacks of using AI as a source of moral guidance.

This lesson was developed by Ewen Glen.

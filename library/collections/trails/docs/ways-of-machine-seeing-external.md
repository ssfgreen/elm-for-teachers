# Ways of machine seeing (External)

This website from London South Bank University holds a collection of activities for Art & Design teachers to share ideas which explore current developments in image-based Generative AI. Their interest is in how both humans and machines are taught to see, in turn how visual culture is being transformed by developments in computer vision.

[Have a look at the site here.](https://waysof.net/machine-seeing/)

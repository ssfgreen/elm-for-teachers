# Dyslexia-friendly checklist

1. Each instruction is one short sentence, active voice, before the task it is for.
2. No paragraph is longer than about four lines; lists are used for steps and options.
3. Headings break the page into parts.
4. Emphasis is bold only: no italics, underlining or block capitals.
5. Subject terms are kept and explained; a word bank is there if the language is new.
6. There is a worked example before the first question.
7. Answer spaces are lines of dots long enough for the answer.
8. The learning goal and the questions' demand are the same as the original.
